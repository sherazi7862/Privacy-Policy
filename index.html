Flokza ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Flokza mobile application ("App").

  • Product information

We only intend to process information that is necessary to provide the application's business-management functionality.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed through Flokza may be used to:

  • Provide and operate the App

  • Maintain and improve the reliability of the App
  • Provide features requested by the organization using Flokza
  • Protect the App and its users from unauthorized access or misuse

Flokza may contain business and transaction information belonging to the organization using the App.

Users are responsible for keeping their login credentials confidential and should not share their passwords or authentication information with unauthorized persons.

If you believe that your account has been accessed without authorization, you should notify the appropriate Flokza administrator.

Flokza is a business management application and is not directed toward children.

We do not knowingly design the App to collect personal information from children.
